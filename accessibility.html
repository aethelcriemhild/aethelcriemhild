Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Commitment to Accessibility

Criemhild, Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards to guarantee that our entire website—including our informational pages and our B2B Wholesale Order Portal—is inclusive, navigable, and highly functional.

Conformance Status

We are actively taking steps and devoting resources to further enhance the accessibility of our website to meet or exceed applicable standards. Our ongoing accessibility efforts work toward conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1, Level AA criteria. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

Feedback and Assistance

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the Criemhild, Inc. website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using our site, or if you require assistance navigating our Wholesale Order Portal or submitting an inquiry, please let us know. We will make all reasonable efforts to address your concerns and provide the information or service you are seeking through an alternative, accessible method.
