2. Personal Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the personal information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Contact & General Inquiries: When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, subject line, the contents of your message, and your acknowledgment of our Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy.
  • Wholesale & B2B Orders: When you use our Criemhild Order Portal, we collect your company name, account type (e.g., Franchise, Distributor, Independent Café), contact person's name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order categories and products of interest, notes you provide (such as estimated volumes, number of locations, delivery window, or voltage requirements), and your confirmation that the request is made on behalf of a registered business.

When you pay a wholesale invoice, payment is processed by the third-party payment processor identified in the invoice, under its own privacy policy, and we receive information such as your company name, invoice and transaction details, and payment status. We may also collect billing details, tax or resale certificate information, and related business records needed to complete the transaction.
  • Other Communications: If you contact us by email, telephone, or otherwise, we collect the information you choose to provide.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics Data (only with your consent): If you click "Accept" on our cookie consent banner, we use Google Analytics 4 ("GA4") to collect information about your interaction with the Website, such as cookie and device identifiers, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring pages, time spent, interactions, and approximate geographic location (for example, city or region). According to Google, GA4 uses your IP address to derive approximate location and does not log or store IP addresses. We do not use GA4 to collect precise geolocation.
  • Server Logs and Technical Data: Our hosting provider, Vercel Inc., automatically records standard server log information needed for security, performance monitoring, and troubleshooting, such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, browser and device information, and request headers.

C. Information from Other Sources

We may receive business contact information about you from our authorized sales representatives, distributors, or business partners, or from publicly available business sources, in connection with wholesale inquiries and orders.

We do not knowingly collect "sensitive personal information" as defined by California law. Please do not submit sensitive personal information (such as government identification numbers or health information) through our forms.

7. Cookies, Local Storage, and Tracking Choices

The Website operates on an "opt-in" basis for non-essential tracking.

  • Analytics Cookies: GA4 scripts and associated cookies are loaded only if you click "Accept" on our cookie consent banner. If you click "Decline" or do not respond to the banner, GA4 cookies are not set and no data is sent to Google Analytics.
  • Changing Your Choice: You may change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the Website footer. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection but does not affect data collected before withdrawal. You may also block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage only to record your cookie consent choice so that you are not asked again on each visit. This entry does not contain your name, email address, or other directly identifying information.
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not use advertising or marketing cookies, and we do not permit third parties to use our Website to track you across other websites for advertising purposes.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" ("DNT") setting. Because we do not load analytics unless you affirmatively consent, and we do not track you across third-party websites over time, the Website operates the same way whether or not your browser sends a DNT signal. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control ("GPC") signal, we treat it as a decision to decline analytics cookies.

  • Cookie consent preference: stored in your browser until you change it or clear your browser storage.
